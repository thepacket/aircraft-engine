Three engines

Pick the engine in the top bar. Turbofan (CFM56-7B26): starter, fuel lever at 25% N2, idle at N1 21%. Piston (O-360): mixture rich, 2-3 primer shots cold, magnetos BOTH, crank; mag check at 1,800 rpm. Turboprop (PT6A-114A): prop lever full, starter, condition lever to LOW IDLE at 12-15% Ng, starter off at idle.

Scenario script

at 0     starter on
at 18    fuel on
when n2 >= 59   note idle reached
at 70    tla 1.0           # 0..1
when n1 >= 95   fault surge
at 100   tla 0
at 120   engine 2          # or 1, all

Timed lines: at <s> <cmd>. Conditional lines: when <signal> <op> <value> <cmd>, armed at the preceding at time, fire once. Signals: n1, n2, egt, ff, thrust, oilp, oilt, vib, t4, sm_hpc, alt, mach… (any chart channel).

Commands: engine 1|2|all, ctl <control> <value> (any control of the engine: tla, mixture, magnetos, power_lever, prop_lever, condition_lever, carb_heat…), starter, fuel on|off (fuel lever / mixture / condition lever), tla x (main lever), n1 pct|off, alt m, mach x, isa dK, humidity x, running level, antiice, pack, reverser, fault name [value], bottle, log on|off, speed x, note text, end.

Faults

starter_inop, starter_weak, starter_early_cutout, ignition_fail, start_fuel_factor (1 = normal), fuel_pump_fail, oil_leak_qt_per_min, fire, n1_governor_fail, compressor_damage_pct, fod, egt_probe_fail, reverser_stuck; one-shots: surge, flameout, fire_bottle.

Replay

Load a CSV flight log, map its columns, and the simulator is driven by the logged levers (or follows the logged N1). Logged channels are overlaid on the charts as dashed lines and the errors are tabulated.
