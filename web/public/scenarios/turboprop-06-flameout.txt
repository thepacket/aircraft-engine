# and the propeller goes to the fine stop. Ignition ON relights it within the
# envelope (Ng above 12%, below 20,000 ft); the sooner it is on, the less Ng
# is lost. Leave ignition on until the engine is back at its power setting.
at 0    alt 2500
at 0    mach 0.25
at 0    ctl prop_lever 1
at 0    running 0.7

at 10   note Flameout: torque zero, Ng decaying, prop on the fine stop
when torque < 50  check shp < 50
when torque < 50  note Ignition ON: relight
when torque < 50  ctl ignition on
when torque >= 400 note Relit and back at the power setting; ignition OFF
when torque >= 400 ctl ignition off
at 60   check ng >= 75
at 60   check beta < 40
at 60   check torque >= 500
at 60   check itt < 600
at 65   end
