# PT6A-42 ground start (King Air B200): prop lever full, starter, condition
# lever to LOW IDLE at 12-15% Ng, watch ITT (start limit 1,000 C), starter
# off at ~52% Ng, low idle Ng ~60%.
at 0    log on
at 0    ctl prop_lever 1
at 2    note Starter ON: Ng motoring, oil pressure rising
at 2    ctl starter on
when ng >= 13   note Condition lever LOW IDLE: light-off in ~2 s, watch ITT
when ng >= 13   ctl condition_lever 1
when ng >= 13   watch itt < 1000
when ng >= 52   ctl starter off
when ng >= 52   note Stabilised at low idle: Ng ~60%, ITT ~450 C
at 65   check ng >= 56
at 65   check oilp >= 60
at 70   ctl condition_lever 2
at 70   note HIGH IDLE: Ng 70%
at 108  check ng >= 68
at 110  end
