# Lycoming O-320-E2D cold start and run-up (Cessna 172N checklist order).
# Watch: cranking ~170 rpm, fire within a few seconds when primed, oil
# pressure within 30 s, then the 1,700 rpm run-up and the magneto check.
at 0    log on
at 0    note Mixture rich, 3 primer shots, magnetos BOTH
at 0    ctl mixture 1
at 2    ctl primer 3
at 4    ctl magnetos 3
at 6    note Crank
at 6    ctl starter on
when rpm >= 500  ctl starter off
when rpm >= 500  note Running: oil pressure must show within 30 s
at 20   ctl throttle 0.2
at 20   note Warm up at ~1,000 rpm until oil temp is in the green
at 50   check oilp >= 25
at 120  speed 5
at 300  speed 1
at 300  note Run-up at ~2,000 rpm: mags L and R (each drops the rpm; max 175 drop, within 50 of each other)
at 300  ctl throttle 0.45
at 300  check oilt >= 60
at 315  check rpm >= 1990
at 318  ctl magnetos 1
at 325  check rpm <= 1990
at 325  check rpm >= 1850
at 326  ctl magnetos 3
at 330  ctl magnetos 2
at 337  check rpm <= 1990
at 337  check rpm >= 1850
at 338  ctl magnetos 3
at 345  ctl throttle 0
at 360  end
