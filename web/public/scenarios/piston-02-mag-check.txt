# Magneto check at ~1,800 rpm. Each magneto alone fires one plug per cylinder,
# so the charge burns a little slower and the rpm drops. Lycoming's limits are
# a MAXIMUM drop of 175 rpm and no more than 50 rpm difference between the two
# magnetos; a drop of 50-150 is typical, and NO drop means a mag is not
# grounding (both still live). Then the same check with a fouled plug, and with
# a dead magneto.
at 0    running 0.35
at 0    log on
at 5    note Both mags: note the rpm
at 5    check rpm >= 1700
at 5    check rpm <= 1880
at 10   note LEFT only: a small, smooth drop
at 10   ctl magnetos 1
at 18   check rpm <= 1810
at 18   check rpm >= 1600
at 20   ctl magnetos 3
at 25   note RIGHT only: about the same drop (within 50 rpm of LEFT)
at 25   ctl magnetos 2
at 33   check rpm <= 1810
at 33   check rpm >= 1600
at 35   ctl magnetos 3
at 45   note Fouled plug on the left-mag side: a big drop and roughness on LEFT (over the 175 limit)
at 45   fault plug_fouled on
at 48   ctl magnetos 1
at 58   check rpm < 1600
at 58   check rough > 0
at 60   ctl magnetos 3
at 65   fault plug_fouled off
at 70   note Dead right magneto: the engine quits on RIGHT alone
at 70   fault mag_right_fail on
at 73   ctl magnetos 2
when rpm < 300  note Engine stopped. Back to BOTH and restart with the starter
when rpm < 300  check firing < 0.5
when rpm < 300  ctl magnetos 3
when rpm < 300  ctl starter on
when rpm >= 600 ctl starter off
when rpm >= 600 note Running on the left magneto alone
at 108  check firing >= 0.5
at 108  check rpm >= 1500
at 110  end
