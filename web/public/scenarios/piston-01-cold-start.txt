# Lycoming O-360 cold start, warm-up and run-up (Piper Archer checklist order).
# Watch: cranking rpm ~170, fire within a few seconds when primed, cold oil
# pressure high, CHT climbing slowly, then the 1,800 rpm run-up.
at 0    log on
at 0    note Mixture rich, fuel pump on, 3 primer shots, magnetos BOTH
at 0    ctl mixture 1
at 0    ctl fuel_pump on
at 2    ctl primer 3
at 4    ctl magnetos 3
at 6    note Crank
at 6    ctl starter on
when rpm >= 500  ctl starter off
when rpm >= 500  note Running: check oil pressure within 30 s
at 15   ctl fuel_pump off
at 20   ctl throttle 0.2
at 20   note Warm up at ~1,000 rpm until oil temp is in the green
at 50   check oilp >= 25
at 120  speed 5
at 300  speed 1
at 300  note Run-up at ~2,000 rpm: carb heat check (rpm drops ~90 with heat on), then idle
at 300  ctl throttle 0.47
at 300  check oilt >= 60
at 315  check rpm >= 2000
at 320  ctl carb_heat on
at 333  note Carb heat: warm, less dense intake air costs rpm (that drop is the check)
at 333  check rpm <= 2020
at 333  check rpm >= 1900
at 335  ctl carb_heat off
at 345  ctl throttle 0
at 360  end
