# Carburettor icing: 10 C, high humidity, partial throttle in cruise. The rpm
# and manifold pressure creep down over minutes. Carb heat clears the ice
# (momentary roughness as the water goes through the engine). The heat stays
# ON while the aircraft is still in icing conditions: switching it off too
# early lets the ice form all over again. Once out of the moist air, heat OFF
# gives the small power loss of the warm intake air back.
at 0    isa 2
at 0    humidity 0.9
at 0    alt 1000
at 0    mach 0.16
at 0    running 0.6
at 0    log on
at 5    note Cruise at 60% throttle in icing conditions. Watch rpm and MAP.
at 5    speed 5
when carb_ice >= 0.3  speed 1
when carb_ice >= 0.3  note RPM dropping: apply carb heat (momentary roughness while the ice clears)
when carb_ice >= 0.3  check rpm < 2330
when carb_ice >= 0.3  ctl carb_heat on
when carb_ice <= 0.02 note Ice cleared, power back. Leave the heat ON: still in icing conditions
when carb_ice <= 0.02 check power_pct >= 55
at 300  note Out of the moist air (humidity 30%): carb heat OFF
at 300  humidity 0.3
at 300  ctl carb_heat off
at 420  check carb_ice < 0.05
at 420  check power_pct >= 60
at 430  end
