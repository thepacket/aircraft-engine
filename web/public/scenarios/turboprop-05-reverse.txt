# Landing roll: touchdown at 65 kt, power lever into beta then max reverse.
# The blades go to -11 deg, Ng rises to ~85%, thrust goes negative, and the
# Np overspeed governor tops the fuel if the prop windmills too fast.
at 0    mach 0.1
at 0    ctl prop_lever 1
at 0    running 0
at 0    log on
at 5    note Touchdown: power lever to beta (flat pitch)
at 5    ctl power_lever 0
at 8    note Max reverse
at 8    ctl power_lever -0.3
at 15   mach 0.06
at 20   mach 0.03
at 25   note Out of reverse below 40 kt (FOD / ground vortex)
at 25   ctl power_lever 0
at 40   end
