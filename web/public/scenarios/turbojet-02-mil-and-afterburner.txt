# Military power and afterburner. Idle to MIL: the nozzle closes as the
# throttle advances, raising EGT and thrust. Past the MIL gate the
# afterburner lights (~1.5 s) and the nozzle opens to pass the reheated gas:
# thrust rises ~45 % while rpm and turbine temperature do not change.
at 0    alt 0
at 0    mach 0
at 0    running 0
at 0    log on
at 5    note Throttle to MIL
at 5    tla 1.0
at 5    watch egt < 735
at 14   check thrust >= 11.3
at 14   check nozzle <= 5
at 20   note Throttle to MAX A/B: nozzle opens, fuel flow triples
at 20   tla 1.3
at 30   check thrust >= 16
at 30   check nozzle >= 60
at 30   check n <= 100.6
at 40   note A/B off, back to idle
at 40   tla 0
at 60   check n <= 51
at 60   check ff_ab < 0.001
at 65   end
