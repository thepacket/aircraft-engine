# PT6A-114A ground start: prop lever full, starter, condition lever to LOW
# IDLE at 12-15% Ng, watch ITT (start limit 1,090 C), starter off at idle.
at 0    log on
at 0    ctl prop_lever 1
at 2    note Starter ON: Ng motoring, oil pressure rising
at 2    ctl starter on
when ng >= 13   note Condition lever LOW IDLE: light-off in ~2 s, watch ITT
when ng >= 13   ctl condition_lever 1
when ng >= 50   ctl starter off
when ng >= 50   note Stabilised at low idle: Ng ~52%, ITT ~500 C, prop turning slowly
at 70   ctl condition_lever 2
at 70   note HIGH IDLE: Ng 65%
at 90   end
