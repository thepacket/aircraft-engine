# Propeller governing: the prop lever sets rpm, the governor changes blade
# angle to hold it. Lower rpm at the same power means more torque. Feather
# stops the prop after a shutdown in flight.
at 0    alt 1500
at 0    mach 0.25
at 0    ctl prop_lever 1
at 0    running 0.7
at 0    log on
at 5    note 1,900 rpm governed. Pull the prop lever to 1,600: blade angle coarsens, torque rises
at 10   ctl prop_lever 0.1
at 30   ctl prop_lever 1
at 45   note Shut down (condition lever CUTOFF) and feather
at 45   ctl condition_lever 0
at 48   ctl prop_lever 0
at 90   note Prop feathered and stopped; unfeather and restart with the starter
at 90   ctl prop_lever 1
at 92   ctl starter on
when ng >= 13   ctl condition_lever 1
when ng >= 50   ctl starter off
at 150  end
