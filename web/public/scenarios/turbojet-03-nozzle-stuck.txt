# Exhaust nozzle stuck closed with the afterburner lit: the reheated gas
# cannot pass the military-power throat, the turbine back-pressure rises and
# the compressor stalls. The EGT gauge lags; the stall itself is the cue.
# Procedure: A/B OFF at once (throttle to MIL). With the nozzle at the MIL
# position and no reheat, the engine recovers and runs normally.
at 0    alt 0
at 0    mach 0
at 0    running 1.0
at 0    log on
at 5    note Nozzle actuator fails at the MIL position
at 5    fault nozzle_stuck on
at 10   note Select MAX A/B: watch the stall margin
at 10   tla 1.3
when stalls >= 1  note COMPRESSOR STALL: A/B OFF, throttle to MIL
when stalls >= 1  tla 1.0
at 25   check stalls >= 1
at 40   check stalls <= 1
at 40   check ff_ab < 0.001
at 40   check n >= 95
at 40   check egt < 700
at 44   check throttle <= 1.0
at 45   end
